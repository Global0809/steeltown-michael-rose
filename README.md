# Steeltown Michael Parfum

A static fragrance website featuring the supplied Pink and Silver 3D bottles in a softly lit editorial composition, Shader Lines in a subtle pink spectrum with its original motion, animated typography, layered campaign photography, film and music. Scanner overlays have been removed. Photo hover transitions, scroll reveals and atmospheric motion respect system reduced-motion settings. Clicking a product opens a live 3D atelier with orbit and zoom, the supplied cap animation, cinematic camera presets, Rosé/Pearl/After dark lighting, automatic rotation, floating 3D petals and a pedestal. One renderer and the exact supplied bottle geometry are shared with the landing page. Hidden/photo views stop rendering, and system reduced-motion settings are respected. Each product gallery still contains five photographs with thumbnails and keyboard navigation.

Published with GitHub Pages from the root of the codex/publish branch. All asset paths are relative, including the locally served Three.js bundle and Draco decoder. No build step is required for this published edition.

Shader Lines is credited to Ali Imam and was supplied by the site owner. Brand assets and media were supplied or commissioned for this website. Third-party runtime notices are included in vendor/.

The supplied PayPal checkout is connected from both product sheets. Catalog values: Eau de parfum, 100 ml, $175 USD, Pink Candy Rose Edition and The Silver Edition. The PayPal page lists a generic Perfume item and does not receive the selected finish; buyers are told to confirm their finish with the seller before paying. Automatic English film captions await final proofreading.
